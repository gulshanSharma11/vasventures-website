About Alok

Ancient wisdom, modern precision

Alok Trivedi is a Jyotirvid, Vastu Visharad and numerology expert based in Noida. For more than 18 years he has studied and practised the Vedic sciences — Jyotish, Vastu Shastra and Numerology — and guided clients across India, the USA, the UK, the UAE, Canada and Australia.

He holds the Jyotish Visharad certification and is a Life Member of the Indian Council of Astrological Sciences (ICAS), Chennai. Alongside astrology, he is also a Reiki healer and palmist.

Alok believes the Vedic sciences deserve the same rigour as any other discipline. That belief led him to build his own Vastu analysis software in 2018, and in 2023 to found VAS Ventures Pvt. Ltd. — bringing trusted guidance to people everywhere through technology with Call-Astro, and extending the same care to families with EyezonKid and Togethr.
