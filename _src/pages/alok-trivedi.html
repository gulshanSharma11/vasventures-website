Founder's message

Why I started VAS Ventures

Every family I know is looking for the same things: clarity when big decisions come up, confidence that their children are safe online, and an easy way to stay close to the people they love.

I started VAS Ventures to build products for exactly those moments. Call-Astro makes trusted astrological guidance available to anyone, anywhere. EyezonKid gives parents peace of mind without taking away a child's privacy. Togethr brings people together in a space that is simple and secure.

We are a young company, and we are building for the long term. Our promise is simple — we will only build products we would trust in our own homes.
