{{crumbs}} About the company

A family of products, built with purpose.

VAS Ventures Pvt. Ltd. is a privately held technology company based in Noida, India. We create and run consumer products for spiritual guidance, children's online safety and community.

Our story

Started with one question.

How can technology help families with the things that matter most — the big decisions, the safety of their children and the relationships that hold them together?

Alok Trivedi founded VAS Ventures to answer that question with real products. With Call-Astro, astrology, Vastu and numerology are online, so people can get guidance from experienced astrologers wherever they are.

With EyezonKid, parents can set healthy screen habits for their children without invading their privacy. And with Togethr, families and communities have a simple, secure place to stay close.

Today, VAS Ventures is the parent company of all three brands. Each product has its own identity, but they share one team, one set of standards and one goal: to make family life calmer, safer and more connected.
