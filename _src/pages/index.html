Who we are

One company. Many ventures.

VAS Ventures was founded by Alok Trivedi with a simple belief: technology should make everyday life calmer, safer and more meaningful for families.

We design, build and run our own consumer products — each with its own brand and team focus, all sharing the same standards for quality, privacy and trust.
